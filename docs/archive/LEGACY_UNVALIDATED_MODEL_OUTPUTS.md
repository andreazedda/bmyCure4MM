> **ARCHIVED — NOT CURRENT BEHAVIOR OR VALIDATED EVIDENCE.**

individual PFS/OS estimates, response/toxicity probabilities, and molecular
database heuristics as if they were current outcome-prediction or validated
evidence capabilities. Those claims are invalid for scientific interpretation.

and molecule-to-simulation bridge pages from the current documentation. Git
history remains the audit source for their exact historical contents. The
underlying legacy research modules, where retained for traceability, are not
authorized patient-specific prediction interfaces.
