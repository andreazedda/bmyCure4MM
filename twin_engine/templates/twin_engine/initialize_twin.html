Research What-if Cockpit

Initialization creates a research twin state from one assessment by mapping available disease and blood markers into the mechanistic model state. This is a reproducible research starting point, not a clinical validation step.

Research simulation only. Initialization is not calibration, not a what-if simulation, not a causal effect estimate, and not clinically validated.

What initialization is

A deterministic conversion from one dated assessment into a PatientTwinState that can be audited and reused.

Assessment(t0) -> risk mapping -> tumor/healthy/immune parameters -> PatientTwinState

What initialization is not

  • Not a fitted calibration result.
  • Not a treatment comparison.
  • Not a clinical diagnosis.
  • Not proof that future simulations are accurate.
